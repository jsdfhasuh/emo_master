3. In staging, compare native push/PR/manual and tag dispatch identities with approved product workflow blobs. Write only optional contexts with exact generation. A posted success needs independently verified matching worker/evidence; a green emitter isn't a worker verdict. Lost writes are reconciled by readback; do not steal an uncertain claim. A verified rejection can retry boundedly, then stops and reports. A no-worker deadline attaches an error to the exact origin run rather than inventing a public run.
4. Only for an approved later product transition, save the existing settings and decide the precise required contexts and source. There are no configured product required checks to claim or to remove. Original source check labels are documented candidates only. Do not enable an official check before its result path is proven.
5. To stop staging, keep the original workflow triggers untouched, turn off staged admission, fence the sole writer before resolving pending generations, and leave or restore settings from the saved configuration. Preserve no-publish and no-visibility-change approval boundaries. Do not remove evidence needed for readback.
